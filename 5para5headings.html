Environment

The environment is everything that surrounds us, including both living and non-living things like air, water, land, plants, and animals. It provides us with the essential resources needed for survival, such as clean air to breathe, safe water to drink, and fertile soil to grow food. However, human activities like pollution, deforestation, and excessive use of resources are damaging our planet. Protecting the environment through simple actions like planting trees, saving water, and reducing waste is vital for a healthy and sustainable future.

Global Warming

Global warming is the steady rise in the Earth's temperature caused by excess greenhouse gases trapped in the atmosphere.

Pollution

Pollution is the harmful addition of waste and toxic substances into our natural environment, threatening both human health and ecosystems.

Soil Erosion

Soil erosion is the natural or human-accelerated process where the top fertile layer of soil is worn away and transported by forces like water, wind, or tillage.

Afforestation

Afforestation is the process of planting trees and establishing forests in areas that did not have any recent tree cover. This practice is essential for fighting climate change because growing trees absorb harmful carbon dioxide from the air and release fresh oxygen.
